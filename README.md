# 🛒 E-commerce Customer Behaviour & Churn Analysis

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze e-commerce customer behavior, identify churn patterns, understand customer engagement, and generate actionable business insights for improving customer retention.

## 📌 Project Overview

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of customer data through purchases, browsing behavior, cart activity, engagement, and device usage. However, raw customer data alone does not provide a clear understanding of why customers churn, which customer groups are at higher risk, or where retention opportunities exist.

This project transforms customer-level data into an interactive Power BI dashboard that helps analyze:

• Customer behavior and engagement

• Customer churn patterns

• Cart abandonment

• Age-group-wise churn behavior

• Device usage patterns

• Customer risk segments

• Retention opportunities

• Business recommendations

The dashboard follows a business-focused analytical approach:

Data → Cleaning → Transformation → Analysis → Visualization → Insights → Recommendations

## 🎯 Problem Statement

The objective of this project is to analyze e-commerce customer behavior and churn patterns using Power BI, identify customer segments with higher churn risk, understand behavioral indicators such as cart abandonment and engagement, and provide actionable recommendations to improve customer retention and engagement.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze overall customer behavior and engagement.

• Measure and understand customer churn.

• Identify customer groups with higher churn risk.

• Analyze cart abandonment patterns.

• Compare churn behavior across age groups.

• Examine customer engagement levels.

• Analyze device usage patterns.

• Segment customers based on behavioral and risk indicators.

• Generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

## 💡 Key Insights : 

The overall churn rate is 28.9%, which indicates a significant loss of customers. Nearly 1 in 3 customers are leaving the platform, signaling the need for immediate retention strategies.

The cart abandonment rate is approximately 57%, showing that more than half of the users drop off before completing a purchase. 

Mobile usage stands at only 16.6%, indicating that the platform is not effectively engaging mobile users. 

Customers with moderate engagement levels are more likely to churn compared to highly engaged users and Customers in the 26–35 and 36–50 age groups show higher churn and risk levels.

## 🚀 Recommendations : 

Improve checkout process to reduce abandonment

Enhance mobile app performance and UX

Target medium-value customers with personalized offers

Launch retention campaigns for at-risk users

Use engagement strategies like notifications and emails
